Privacy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This page explains the current information practices for JeffersonWM Home and the public JeffersonWM pages that link here.

Essential account sessions

Some JeffersonWM tools use the central Auth service to sign people in and keep an authenticated session active. That service uses an essential session cookie so it can recognize a signed-in account, protect account-only actions, and support sign out and session revocation. It is not used for optional analytics.

Optional analytics

JeffersonWM Home, Feed, and Project Activity currently use Google Analytics to understand aggregate use of those public pages. Consent controls are being consolidated across the JeffersonWM domain and will gate that optional analytics once they are in place. When a page presents an analytics prompt, choosing No leaves optional analytics unloaded.

Browser preferences

Some pages may store a small preference in the browser, such as an interface setting or a remembered consent choice. These values help the page remember a selection on the same browser. They can be removed through the browser's site-data controls.

Service providers

Requests to JeffersonWM may be handled by the hosting and network providers that deliver the site. When analytics is accepted, Google Analytics receives the information described by its service. Those providers may process technical request information, such as browser, device, and network data, as part of delivering their services.

Your choices

  • Choose No when an analytics prompt appears to keep optional analytics off for that page.
  • Use your browser's controls to clear cookies and site storage or adjust browser privacy settings.
  • Sign out of an account when you are finished using account-only tools.

Updates

This page will be updated when JeffersonWM's data practices materially change. The date above shows the latest revision.
